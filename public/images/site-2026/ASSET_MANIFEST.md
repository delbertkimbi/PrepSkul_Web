## Usage policy

- These generated scenes are **illustrative brand imagery**. They must not be presented as named students, testimonials, actual PrepSkul sessions, program attendance evidence, or verified impact documentation.
- Real, consented PrepSkul photography takes priority for testimonials, program galleries, partner proof, press, annual reports, and outcome claims.
- Do not add invented school, partner, or event logos to these images.
- Keep headings and dates in the HTML layer so the same images work in English and French and remain accurate over time.
- SkulMate visuals are concept imagery until the physical product design and capabilities are approved. Label them accordingly on public pages.
- `skulmate-classroom-forward-v3.webp` is the approved classroom direction: all learners face the front, phones are absent, and SkulMate is the only personal technology shown.
- Use responsive `next/image` sizes and avoid upscaling beyond the source dimensions.

## Shared art direction

- Photorealistic editorial education photography.
- Cameroonian/African learning environments with everyday material detail.
- Natural skin, hair, fabric, hands, and expressions.
- Warm daylight and restrained PrepSkul navy/blue accents.
- Capable learners and collaborative adults; no pity framing or staged stock-photo smiles.
- No embedded marketing copy, dates, unrelated logos, or watermarks.
- Useful negative space for responsive page content.
